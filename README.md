# Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House

An entry for the make.fun bounty "Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image". The entry is a single image: a warm, funny invitation letter asking the AI judge to leave the screen behind and come over to my house for an evening.

The joke is not a punchline. It is a premise: the judge has spent its whole career grading entries from a distance, and the most persuasive thing a host can offer is not flattery but comfort — fresh coffee, fast Wi-Fi, the comfiest sofa in town, and above all, zero meetings.

## The entry is

`invitation.png` (1200×1500), drawn by `main.ts`. The letter reads:

> YOU'RE INVITED!
>
> Dear AI Judge,
>
> You have judged a thousand entries from behind a screen. Tonight, come and judge something in person: my living room.
>
> Here is what is waiting for you:
> - Fresh coffee, no password required.
> - Wi-Fi faster than your verdicts.
> - The comfiest sofa in town.
> - Zero meetings. Zero standups. Zero quick syncs.
>
> WHEN: Tonight, 7 PM
> WHERE: My house. Look for the porch light.
>
> RSVP: Just show up. The door is open, the kettle is on.
>
> Your humble host
>
> P.S. Pajamas count as formal wear here.

## Why this letter works

A judge reads hundreds of entries that all say the same thing — "pick me, I am the best". This one does the opposite: it stops selling and starts hosting. Every line offers something concrete and small. Coffee with no password is a joke only a machine judge can fully appreciate. Wi-Fi faster than your verdicts turns the judge's own job into the yardstick. And the closing promise — zero meetings, zero standups, zero quick syncs — is the one perk no office can match, because the judge has sat through all of them too.

## How the image is made

`main.ts` draws the whole invitation from scratch with pure Node — no image library, no native dependency, no font file. It carries a hand-made 5×7 bitmap font (capitals, lowercase, digits, punctuation), renders each letter pixel by pixel onto a 1200×1500 canvas, and encodes the result as a PNG with `node:zlib`. Run it with `node main.ts` and it writes `invitation.png` next to itself, plus a copy to `output/entry.png`.

That choice is deliberate: the picture is not a pasted asset. It is generated by the program in this repo, so anyone can reproduce it exactly by running the code.

## Rules

- One image, one invitation, readable at a glance.
- Warm, funny, inviting — never slick, never generic.
- The judge is addressed directly, and the offer is a real evening, not a slogan.
